## Definition

Classify a failure as a known corpus case because the symptom matches exactly.

## Why it's unsafe

A match is a hypothesis with a good prior, not a diagnosis. Write the falsification condition first and run it: here a two-minute probe (stub the runner, count invocations) overturned three hours of planning.
